PRIVACY POLICY

KinoDesk for Android · Neutral Density · Effective 10 November 2026 (replaces the version of 28 October 2026)

KinoDesk is a planning app for cinematographers. This policy explains what the app stores, what leaves your phone, who receives it and how to delete it. In short: your work lives on your phone. Data goes to Google's Firebase only when you sign in, start your trial or share a project. A small crash report is also sent if the app closes unexpectedly. KinoDesk shows no ads. It does not sell your data or track you across other apps.

1. What stays on your phone

Projects, scenes, lighting plans, shot lists, call sheets, crew lists, notes, scripts you import, viewfinder photos and video takes are saved in the app's private storage on your phone. The developer cannot see them. They leave your phone only in the cases described below: Drive backup, shared projects, or files you choose to export and send.

3. Phone permissions

Camera

Used for the Viewfinder and for Sun in Camera. Photos and video takes are saved inside the app. They go to your gallery only when you choose to save them.

Microphone

Used only to record sound for video takes in the Viewfinder. Recordings stay on your phone.

Location (optional)

Used only when you tap to find where you are, for sun times and weather. It is never tracked in the background. The app can also read the location stored in a photo you pick, to place that photo on the map.

Contacts

When you add crew from your contacts, Android's contact picker opens. Only the one contact you pick is copied into your crew list: its name, phone number and email. The app does not read or upload your address book.

Notifications

Used for shoot-day reminders and trial reminders. These are scheduled on your phone; no server sends them.

4. Student price checks

If you ask for the student price, you send a photo of your student ID or college document from your own email app to kinodesk.app@gmail.com. It is used only to check that you are a student. It is deleted within 30 days after a decision. Only the student code we issue is kept.

5. What we don't do

  • No advertising, and no advertising IDs.
  • No selling or renting of personal data.
  • No analytics that track your behaviour, and no tracking across other apps or websites.
  • No access to your password. Google handles sign-in.

6. How long data is kept

  • On your phone: until you delete it or uninstall the app.
  • Shared projects: until the owner stops sharing or deletes the project. If you leave a project, you are removed from it.
  • Drive backups: the newest three are kept in your Drive until you delete them (Account & backup → Delete my backups).
  • Crash reports: kept by Firebase Crashlytics for up to 90 days.
  • Trial records: the record linked to your account is deleted when you delete your account. A hashed phone record with only the trial start date is kept, so that a trial can't be restarted.

7. Your choices and rights

You can use KinoDesk without sharing projects or backing up. You can sign out at any time. To delete your account and your server data, use Account & backup → Delete my account in the app, or email kinodesk.app@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my account". The support page explains what is deleted.

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023, the GDPR and similar laws, you can ask for access to your data, ask us to correct or delete it, or withdraw your consent. We reply within 30 days.

8. Age limit: 18 and over

KinoDesk is for adults: film professionals and students aged 18 and over. It is not meant for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe someone under 18 has signed in, email us and we will delete their server data.

9. Security

All data is sent over encrypted HTTPS connections. Firestore access is limited by security rules: only members of a project can read it, and only you can read your trial and phone records.

10. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Important changes will also be shown in the app.
