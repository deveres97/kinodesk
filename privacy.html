PRIVACY

KinoDesk privacy policy · effective 28 October 2026

KinoDesk ("the app") is made by Neutral Density, an independent developer in Kerala, India. This page explains what the app does with your information. In short: your work stays on your phone, there are no ads and no tracking, and nothing is sold.

What stays on your phone

Projects, lighting plans, shot lists, call sheets, notes and photos you take in the app are stored only inside the app on your device. We (the developer) have no server and cannot see them.

Camera

Used for the Viewfinder and Sun in Camera. Pictures are saved inside the app, or to your gallery only when you tap save. Nothing is uploaded.

Location (optional)

Used only when you tap to find where you are, so the app can work out sun times and fetch the weather. It is not tracked in the background and not sent to us. To get a forecast, the project's map coordinates (nothing else) are sent to Open-Meteo. The app can also read the location stored inside a photo you pick, to place it on the map.

Google sign-in and Drive backup (optional)

If you choose to sign in with Google, the app asks for permission to (1) see your Google account email address, to show which account your backups use, and (2) store files in a hidden app folder in your own Google Drive (the "appDataFolder"). Backups of your projects go there directly from your phone. Only KinoDesk can open that folder; we do not receive a copy. Your password is never seen by the app. You can delete every backup from the app (Settings → Account & backup → Delete my backups) and remove access any time at Google Account → Security → Third-party apps. KinoDesk's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Purchases

KinoDesk Pro is sold through Google Play. Payment is handled by Google; the app only learns whether you own Pro. We never see your card or bank details. See Google's privacy policy.

Paintings on the Home page

The app asks the Art Institute of Chicago's open API for a public-domain painting. Nothing about you is sent.

What we don't do

No advertising, no analytics or tracking SDKs, no selling or sharing of personal data, no account on our side.

Children

KinoDesk is a professional tool for adults and is not directed at children under 13.

Deleting your data

Delete a project in the app to delete its plans and photos. Uninstalling the app deletes everything on the phone. Drive backups can be deleted in the app or from your Google Drive settings. For any request, email us and we'll reply within 30 days.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
