The app

KinoDesk is a planning tool for cinematographers and filmmakers: lighting plans, exposure estimates, a viewfinder, sun and weather information, shot lists, call sheets and PDFs. You may use it for personal and professional work.

Estimates, not guarantees

Exposure figures (T-stops, foot-candles), beam shapes, field of view, sun positions and weather forecasts are calculated estimates based on published data and your inputs. Real results depend on equipment, conditions and settings. Always check with a light meter and on-set tests.
