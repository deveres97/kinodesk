PRICING

KinoDesk Pro · sold only through Google Play · prices include taxes where Google Play charges them

Every new user gets a 14-day free trial with everything open. No card is needed, and you are never charged automatically when it ends. After the trial, your projects stay readable. Editing, new projects, clean (unwatermarked) PDF exports and CSV exports need KinoDesk Pro.

Google Play shows the price in your local currency. As a guide, it is about US$4.99 a month or US$49.99 a year. The student yearly plan is about US$24.99. The exact price, including any lifetime offer, is shown in the app before you pay.

What Pro includes

  • Unlimited projects and scenes
  • Diagram Master with live T-stops for every light
  • Viewfinder captures, video takes and shot lists
  • Call sheets, gear lists and print-ready PDF exports without watermarks
  • Live shared projects with your crew
  • Backup to your own Google Drive
