REFUND & CANCELLATION POLICY

Effective 10 November 2026

Try before you pay

KinoDesk gives every new user a 14-day free trial with every feature open and no card. Please use the trial to check that the app suits your work before you buy.

Cancelling a subscription

All payments go through Google Play. To cancel, open Google Play → Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → KinoDesk Pro → Cancel subscription. You can also use the Manage subscription button on the Pro page in the app.

  • Cancelling stops the next renewal. Pro stays active until the end of the period you have paid for.
  • Uninstalling the app does not cancel a subscription.
  • No cancellation fee is charged.

Refunds

  • Within 48 hours of a purchase: you can ask Google directly at Google Play → Request a refund. Google decides these requests under its own policy.
  • After 48 hours: email kinodesk.app@gmail.com with your Google Play order number (it starts with GPA.) and the reason. We reply within 2 working days.
  • We will refund a charge that was made by mistake, a duplicate charge, a renewal you cancelled before the renewal date, or a purchase that left Pro not working (when we cannot fix it).
  • Subscriptions: we normally refund the most recent charge if you ask within 7 days of it. Earlier periods that were already used are not refunded.
  • Lifetime purchase: can be refunded within 7 days of purchase.
  • Approved refunds are paid back to the original payment method through Google Play, usually within 5–10 working days, depending on your bank.
  • When a purchase is refunded, Pro is removed from your account.

This policy does not take away any rights you have under the consumer law of the country you live in.
