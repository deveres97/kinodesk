Help with KinoDesk

Email kinodesk.app@gmail.com — tell us your phone model and what happened. We usually reply within two days.

Signing in

KinoDesk uses one Google sign-in for everything: the free trial, shared projects and backups to your Google Drive. Sign in on the welcome page, from the Pro page, or from ••• (Settings) → Account & backup. KinoDesk never sees your password.

The free trial

Sign in with Google and everything opens for 14 days, no card needed. The trial is once per Google account and once per phone, so reinstalling the app or using another Google account on the same phone doesn't start it again. After the trial your projects stay safe and readable; editing, CSV export and clean PDFs need KinoDesk Pro.

I bought Pro on another phone

Sign in to Google Play with the same account, open KinoDesk → Settings → KinoDesk Pro → Restore purchases.

Cancel a subscription

Google Play → profile picture → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → KinoDesk → Cancel. Pro stays on until the end of the period you paid for.

Student price

Film students can get a student code. Email us from your college email, or attach your student ID, and we'll send a code to enter in KinoDesk Pro.

Shared projects

Open a project and tap the people button at the top right to invite an editor or a viewer. They install KinoDesk from Google Play, open My projects → Join a shared project and type the code (like ABCD-2345). Changes and photo previews show on everyone's phone within seconds; videos stay on the phone that shot them. The project owner can reset the invite codes from the people button at any time. Sharing needs KinoDesk Pro or the free trial, for everyone in the project.

Moving to a new phone

On the old phone: Settings → Account & backup → Sign in with Google → Back up now. On the new phone: install KinoDesk and sign in with the same Google account — your projects come back.

Delete your account and data

In the app: open KinoDesk → ••• (Settings) → Delete my account & data → Delete, then confirm with your Google account.

By email: write to kinodesk.app@gmail.com from the Google account you use in KinoDesk, with the subject "Delete my account". We delete it within 30 days.

What's deleted: your name and email on our servers (Google Firebase), your account's free-trial record, the shared copies and photo previews of projects you own, and your place in projects others shared with you.

What's kept: only a record of when the free trial started on this phone (no name or email), so the trial can't be started again. Projects saved on your phone stay until you uninstall the app. Drive backups stay in your own Google Drive until you delete them there or in Settings → Account & backup.

See also the privacy policy.
